# Support for Capture by TyD

In Capture 1.4.0, choose **Feedback** in the sidebar to send a problem report or suggestion. You can also use [Contact Capture](https://api.capturebytyd.com/report) in your browser. No account or email is required; these forms cannot send a direct reply. Keep your report reference if you need to add information later.

To report a shared capture, choose **Report this link** on its page and describe the concern. Do not upload the content again as evidence. Reporting does not automatically remove content.

For a public discussion, you can [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/TyD1732/capture-legal/issues). Issues are public: never include private links, clips, logs, credentials, or identifying details.

- [Privacy policy](https://api.capturebytyd.com/privacy)
- [Sharing guidelines](https://api.capturebytyd.com/sharing-rules)

Capture is publicly available through Microsoft Store. Any private test flight is separate from the public release and applies only to its invited testers.
