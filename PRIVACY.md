# Privacy Policy for Capture by TyD

Publisher: YSProductions / TyD

The current [Capture privacy policy](https://api.capturebytyd.com/privacy) explains local recording, optional one-hour hosted sharing, private feedback, retention, security, and your choices. It replaces the earlier policy describing user-managed storage only.

Capture does not automatically upload your recordings. When you choose to create a sharing link, Capture hosts a copy for one hour; anyone with the link can view or download it. Access expiry cannot recall downloaded copies, and physical deletion can be delayed during outages.

Feedback includes your submitted text and technical details only when you choose to include them. No clip, audio, screenshot or log is attached automatically. Reports are scheduled for removal after 90 days, with encrypted backup history for 14 days; failed jobs or outages can delay removal. See the full policy for details.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use [Contact Capture](https://api.capturebytyd.com/report). No account or email is required; the form cannot send a direct reply. Keep your report reference. Do not post private links, recordings, credentials, or personal information in a public GitHub issue.
